# Slackboard Branding

## Name

**Slackboard**: an open-source alternate client for Tension Board.

- Stylized lowercase in repos, packages, and CLI: `slackboard`
- Title case in prose: Slackboard
- Pun: *slack* is the opposite of *tension*. *Slackboard* also sounds like *blackboard*, which fits a board covered in chalk-style problems.
- Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tension Board. Do not use "Tension" in the name or logo.

## Tagline

Primary: **Less tension. More sending.**

Alternates:
- Cut the app some slack.
- The board, minus the tension.
- A client that doesn't fight you.

## Logo concept

A blackboard (chalkboard) with a hand-drawn chalk look:

- A dark slate rectangle with a rounded wooden-style frame, or no frame for the minimal version.
- The wordmark `slackboard` is written in chalk, lowercase.
- A **sagging rope or line** is drawn in chalk across or under the wordmark. It hangs loose in a catenary curve to show slack.
- A few chalk "holds" (small blobs and crimp shapes) are scattered around, with one or two lit in the accent color to suggest board LEDs.
- A chalk smudge or eraser mark sits in a corner.

Variants:
- **Full**: slate board, chalk wordmark, sagging line, holds.
- **Mark only**: a sagging chalk curve ending in two dots, with the dots as anchor points. Used for the app icon and favicon.
- **Mono**: chalk-white on transparent, or black on white, for READMEs and print.

## Naming conventions

| Thing | Name |
|---|---|
| Repo | `slackboard` |
| Package / binary | `slackboard` |
| Repository | `N3rdL0rd/slackboard` |
| Bundle ID suggestion | `app.slackboard.client` |

## Usage notes

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "s" in the wordmark.
- Do not put the logo on a light, busy background. Use the mono variant instead.
- Do not use Tension Board's logos. Use your own assets or open licensed ones.
- Include a disclaimer in the README: "Slackboard is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tension Climbing."
