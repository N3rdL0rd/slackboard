# Code of Conduct

Climbing communities work because people look after each other. This project asks the same.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Criticise code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers, including people who are new to climbing, to open source, or to this code.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never share someone else's data, credentials or location.
- Do not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone for who they are.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's
private reporting on the repository or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.
