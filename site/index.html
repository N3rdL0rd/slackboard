  • One set of stats

    Grades from every board are converted to one scale, so your progress and working grade cover all of them.

  • Suggestions from all boards

    Suggested climbs come from every board you use, and no single board fills more than about 60% of the list.

  • Circuits with any climb

    A circuit can hold climbs from any board. Climbs a board's service can't store stay on your phone.

  • Quick board switching

    Boards in the same place are grouped, and each one keeps its own angle and filters.

Each brand keeps its own account, and nothing is shared between services: the combining happens on your phone. MoonBoard works without an account.

Everything it does

See the board

Board photos from your board's own service, mirrored boards, multi-frame routes with playback, pinch to zoom, and swipe to the next climb.

Light up the board

Send any climb to your board over Bluetooth, with auto-connect and a tray that opens from the climb screen. Works with Tension and MoonBoard controllers; Decoy and Grasshopper use the same protocol.

Log offline

Sends and attempts are saved on your phone and uploaded when you are back online. Edit or delete them any time.

Get suggestions

Climbs picked from your own sends across all your boards: a warm-up, climbs at your level, one to reach for, and a target to try on the gym wall.

Set a climb

Set your own boulders and routes, save drafts, publish, or copy someone else's climb as a starting point. MoonBoard problems are set locally.

Log gym and outdoor climbs

Log gym problems and outdoor boulders with wall angle, style and grade, or repeat a recent one in a single tap. Pick any V grade from V0 up, coloured the way the app colours grades.

See your progress

Working grade, hardest send, sends per week and a progression chart, with a widget on the Climb tab.

Look back at what you lit

Every climb you light up is kept in a lit history, by day and by board, so you can find that climb again.

Circuits and workouts

Build circuits with climbs from any board, follow people and browse theirs, and generate a workout from templates.

Find your gym

Pick your gym on a map and its board is added with the right size, holds and angle. Boards in the same place group together.

Share a climb

Save a clean image of any climb, or a Flashed! card after a first-try send, with confetti for a new hardest grade.

Training timer

Rest and interval timers with a get-ready countdown, and haptic feedback on swipes, sends and the angle dial.

Your data

Stored on your phone

Your climbs and logs live in a database on your phone and work without a signal. Sign-in tokens stay in the phone's keystore.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, and no Slackboard account. It talks to the service of the board you sign in to. The only other requests are the gym map's tiles and board photos.

Open source

MIT licensed. Back everything up to one file and restore it, or export your logbook as a spreadsheet.
