Arrow keys
Move between holds. Number keys tag the selected hold and move on. Backspace clears it.
Jug
Big, positive, whole hand wraps it. You can rest on it.
Incut edge
A ledge with a lip; fingers hook behind it, deeper than a fingertip pad.
Crimp
A thin edge, about one finger pad deep or less.
Sloper
Rounded, no lip; friction and body position.
Pinch
You squeeze it, thumb against fingers.
Pocket
A hole for one to three fingers.
Volume or other
Large surface feature, or anything that does not fit above.
Foot only
A chip or dot meant only for feet.
